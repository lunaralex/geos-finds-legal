Privacy Policy — Geo's Finds Autoposter

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Geo's Finds Autoposter ("the App") is a personal-use automation that publishes Amazon affiliate product content to social media accounts that I personally own and control. It is not a public product and is not used by, or on behalf of, any other user.

What data the App accesses

  • OAuth tokens for my own Pinterest and TikTok accounts, used only to publish posts and read my own account/board information.
  • Post content the App creates (product images, titles, descriptions, and affiliate links), published to my own accounts.

What the App does not do

  • It does not access, read, or store data belonging to any other user of Pinterest or TikTok.
  • It does not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose any data to third parties.
  • It does not use any platform data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating this single automated posting workflow.

Data storage

Authentication tokens and posting history are stored in a private, locally-hosted database used only by this application. Data is not transmitted anywhere other than to Pinterest's and TikTok's own APIs.
